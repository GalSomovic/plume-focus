Privacy policy

Plume Focus · Last updated 26 September 2026

The short version: Plume collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer never receives your data.

What stays on your device

Your focus sessions, the events you add, and the colours you earn are stored on your iPhone or iPad. If iCloud is on, they sync through your own private iCloud database, which only you can access. You can delete everything by deleting the app.

Calendar

With your permission, Plume reads the calendars on your device to show your events and remind you when they start. Calendar data is read on the device and is never sent anywhere.

Screen Time

With your permission, Plume uses Apple’s Screen Time framework to block the apps and websites you choose during a session. iOS keeps that selection private: Plume itself cannot see which apps you picked, or how you use them.

Notifications and alarms

Notifications, Live Activities and alarms are scheduled on your device by iOS. No notification content is sent through any server.

Contacting us

If you email a bug report, we receive what you choose to send, such as your email address, the app and iOS versions, and your message. It is used only to reply and fix the problem, and it is never shared or sold.

Children

Plume does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
